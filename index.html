Privacy Policy — Where Did I Put It?

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Developer: JRWaniTech

1. Introduction

JRWaniTech ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Where Did I Put It? mobile application ("the App") handles information when you use it. The App helps you record personal belongings and remember where you keep them.

2. Information You Enter

You can use the App to save information such as:

  • Item names and categories
  • Locations, specific places, and containers
  • Notes about your belongings
  • Whether an item is marked as important
  • Item creation and update timestamps
  • Photos you choose to attach to items

These details may contain personal or sensitive information, depending on what you choose to record.

3. Local Storage

The App stores item records in a local database on your device. Photos you select are copied into the App's private storage. The current implementation does not require you to create an account to use these features.

4. Photos

The App uses Android's photo-selection functionality so you can choose images to attach to your items. Selected images are copied into the App's private storage for use within the App. You choose which photos to attach.

5. Voice Entry

The App offers voice entry to help you enter an item's name and location. Android's speech-recognition functionality converts your speech into text, which the App then processes to suggest an item name and location.

Depending on your device, settings, and selected recognition service, audio may be processed by a third-party service. That service's handling of audio and related information is governed by its own privacy policy and terms. Please consult the applicable provider's information for details.

You can review and edit the recognized information before saving it.

6. Manual Backup and Restore

The App allows you to export item records and available photos into a ZIP backup file at a location you choose. A backup may contain details about your belongings and where they are kept.

The backup functionality reviewed does not encrypt the ZIP file. Store backups securely and share them only with people you trust. Restoring a backup may replace or update existing records, depending on matching item IDs and the restore process.

Backup files are stored separately from the App's normal data. Deleting an item from the App does not automatically delete copies in previously created backup files.

7. Android Automatic Backup

The App's Android backup configuration contains rules intended to exclude its app data from Android-managed cloud backup and device-transfer backup. Device manufacturers and Android versions may differ in how certain transfer mechanisms operate. We therefore do not guarantee that every possible system-level migration mechanism is disabled.

8. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

The app-level source code and declared dependencies reviewed to date do not show an explicit advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK. The App does use Android's speech-recognition functionality, which may involve a service provider outside JRWaniTech.

The provider's handling of audio and recognized speech depends on the service and your device settings. This policy will be updated if the App's data practices or third-party services change.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Item records and photos remain in the App's local storage until you delete them, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App, subject to Android behavior and any copies you have exported or transferred.

Manual backup files remain wherever you saved or shared them. You are responsible for deleting those copies when they are no longer needed.

10. Security

The App uses Android's app-private storage for its local item data and selected photos. However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Manual ZIP backups are not encrypted by the backup functionality reviewed. Protect these files appropriately.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you have a privacy concern relating to a child's use of the App, please contact us using the details below. The App's intended audience and applicable legal requirements should be considered when publishing this policy.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will include an updated date.
